Section 0
Headline Findings — July 2026

🔍 Critical Insights — Act on These

⚠️ Only 5 posts in July — lowest month of the year. Volume dropped 44% from June (9 posts) and 77% from July 2025 (22 posts). Despite this, per-post impressions hit 6,331 — a 104% improvement over June's 3,105/post. Fewer posts, better reach per post.
The two document/advisor story posts drove 18.8% and 14.6% CTR respectively — consistent with every prior month. "Your experience doesn't have to sit in one company" (Jul 7) generated 542 clicks from 2,882 impressions. This format is reliably the highest-converting asset on the Connectd page.
Instagram carousels outperformed images again: 7.42% avg ER vs 2.97% for images. The "Two decades in one industry" carousel (Jul 14) drove 8.82% ER and was the top-performing post on Instagram this month. Only 2 of 5 Instagram posts were carousels — the format with the most consistent results.
LinkedIn shares jumped to 164 total — double June's 82 — driven largely by the automation/HubSpot post (50 shares) and the Two decades document (45 shares). High share rates on image posts correlate with content that's broadly relevant to a professional audience, not just Connectd followers.
⚠️ Follower growth slowed: 1,014 new followers vs 1,504 in June (−32.6%). With only 5 posts and no sponsored content, the growth slowdown is expected — but per-post follower acquisition (202.8) was higher than June (167.1). The issue is post volume, not content quality.
Actuate Global is the most active competitor this month: 59 posts, 1,247 total engagements, 6.1% follower growth rate. Their eng/post (21.1) is lower than Connectd's (36.6), but their volume is 10× higher. Watch this account — aggressive posting with reasonable engagement is a signal they're investing in content.

🌱 Trend Gaps — 5 Specific Opportunities
📱
Instagram carousels averaged 7.42% ER vs 2.97% for images — for the third consecutive month, carousels outperform all other formats. You only posted 2 carousels out of 5 Instagram posts this month.
Action: Make carousels the default Instagram format. Repurpose the LinkedIn advisor story documents as Instagram carousels — the content is already made.
📅
5 LinkedIn posts in July — the lowest volume since tracking began. At 6,331 impressions per post, posting 9 times (June's volume) would have yielded ~57,000 impressions. That's a 79% impression uplift for the same content quality.
Action: Build a batch content session for August to get back to 9–12 posts. Advisor spotlight documents and event promos are already your two highest-performing formats.
🎯
Document posts drove 544 avg clicks vs 61 for images — an 8.9× gap. Connectd has a consistent high-CTR format but only used it for 2 of 5 July posts. These posts are also your best follower acquisition driver.
Action: Every month should include at least 3 document posts. Advisor profiles, NED tools, and founder spotlights all convert well in this format.
🚀
Actuate Global posted 59 times in July with 1,247 total engagements — their total engagement exceeded all other tracked competitors. Their eng/post (21.1) is lower than Connectd's (36.6), but sustained high volume compounds. They're growing 6.1%/month from a small base.
Action: Monitor Actuate Global's content themes — they're in an adjacent niche and growing fast. Understanding what content is driving their volume is worth 20 minutes of research.
👤
Instagram followers are static at 2,112 — unchanged across all tracked months. The account is reaching 4.3% of followers per post on average but generating no net follower growth.
Action: Add a follow CTA to every Instagram post caption. Test hashtag stacks (#FractionalCXO, #StartupAdvisor, #PortfolioCareer) to reach non-followers. One carousel per week with a strong hook would drive follower acquisition.
