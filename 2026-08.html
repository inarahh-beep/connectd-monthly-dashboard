Section 0
Headline Findings — August 2026

🔍 Critical Insights — Act on These

Volume and quality both up. 17 LinkedIn posts (vs 5 in July, +240%) drove impressions from 31,653 to 42,631 (+34.7%). Per-post impressions fell from 6,331 to 2,508 — the expected trade-off, and the right one: 11k more total impressions for the same content quality already being produced.
Document posts are in a different league. The Aug 27 "Going Fractional" document hit 40.17% ER and 37.15% CTR — the highest ER of any post tracked this year. All 7 document posts averaged 23.39% ER and 21.4% CTR vs 5.06% ER and 3.67% CTR for videos. The format advantage held across every single document post without exception.
Instagram carousels significantly outperform reels: 8.45% avg ER vs 3.68%. Aug 27 led at 11.67% ER, Aug 19 at 9.21%, Aug 12 at 8.14%. Despite this, 10 of 16 IG posts were reels — the consistently lower-performing format. The format with the best results is still the minority format.
Instagram reach surged: views up 243% (1,048 → 3,598) and reach up 423% (456 → 2,386). The NED calculator reel (Aug 13) drove 451 views and 336 reach — the month's most-viewed post. More posts plus stronger captions are clearly working for discoverability.
⚠️ LinkedIn shares fell sharply — 67 total vs 164 in July (−59%) — despite 3× more posts. Per-post shares dropped from 32.8 to 3.94. This isn't a reach problem: it's a content-type problem. Broad professional-interest posts (automation, events, tools) drive shares. August was 100% advisor-niche content, which drives clicks and ER but not virality.
Jack & Jill's 7,102 new followers from 8 posts looks alarming at face value, but the growth is not an organic content signal. They ran 79 paid creatives on LinkedIn in August alone, their employee base has large personal followings and actively reposts company content, and their company page engagement itself is low. This is a paid media + employee advocacy story, not a posting strategy to replicate. No action required on Connectd's organic content approach as a result.

✅ EXP 2 VALIDATED — VIDEO WINS

The talking-head video (Exp 2) outperformed the graphic (Exp 1) on both platforms. On LinkedIn, video hit 7.01% CTR vs. the graphic's 3.92% CTR — nearly 2× higher. On Instagram the gap was smaller (1.49% vs 1.31% ER) but still in video's favour. Exp 1 hypothesis not validated — question-led graphic did not convert better than the narrated video.

💡 LEARNING FOR SEPTEMBER

Talking-head video that names and dismantles specific objections drives higher CTR than a static graphic with a question-led hook. For calculator promotion, lead with video. The graphic format alone is not enough to convert — pair it with video or use it as a retargeting asset.

Section 7
⚡ Biggest Opportunity for September

🎯 THREE THINGS TO DO DIFFERENTLY IN SEPTEMBER

Do more carousels on Instagram, fewer reels. 5 carousels averaged 8.45% ER vs 10 reels at 3.68%. Every carousel in August performed above the account average. The format with the best outcome is still the minority format. At a minimum, the carousel-to-reel split should be 50/50 in September. Aim for 7 carousels and 5–6 reels.
Post one "broad professional interest" LinkedIn document every two weeks. July's viral content (automation, board economics, the future of work) had 32.8 shares per post. August averaged 3.94 shares per post with 100% advisor-niche content. Documents already work — the issue is topic selection. One broad-appeal document every fortnight alongside the advisor content will restore share velocity without sacrificing the audience-building content that's proven to drive clicks and ER.
Spend 30 minutes benchmarking Jack & Jill's content before September starts. Their 7,102 new followers from 8 posts vs Connectd's 835 from 17 posts is the single most important competitive signal this month. Either they ran paid promotion (likely on at least some posts — worth checking their page for Promoted badges) or they posted content that the algorithm picked up for distribution. Either way, the answer is a 30-minute content audit and a hypothesis to test in September.
