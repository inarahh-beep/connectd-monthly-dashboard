Section 0
Headline Findings — March 2026

🔍 Critical Insights — Act on These

March was Connectd's most active month — 23 LinkedIn posts + 11 Instagram posts = 34 total pieces of content. Despite the volume, avg LinkedIn ER held at 15.2% and avg Instagram ER at 5.63%, both strong for the frequency.
Two major press announcements in one month: FT 1000 Europe's Fastest-Growing Companies (#67, Mar 3) and Sifted 100 (Mar 18). The FT post drove 94 likes and 14 reposts — the most reshared single post in March — but only 2.0% CTR, suggesting press content gets shared but not clicked.
The Mar 31 Document "Most advisory roles don't come from a job description" hit 50.1% CTR — matching the dataset record (Sep 1 also hit 50.1%). This is the second time this exact content format (advisor spotlight with a CTA to book a call) has crossed 50% CTR.
March had a US event push (Tampa Mar 17, Miami Mar 19, Dallas Mar 10, NYC recap Mar 19). The Florida events drove 4 LinkedIn posts, generating strong follower spikes — 149 new followers on Mar 26 alone, the highest single-day count this quarter.
Instagram performed consistently better in March than subsequent months: 11 posts, avg ER 5.63%, 5 saves, 9 shares. The Sifted 100 carousel (13.04% ER) and Times Square carousel (10.93%) show that milestone/event content drives the highest Instagram engagement.

🌱 Trend Gaps — Opportunities for Next Month
Based on dataset patterns and niche benchmarks. Updated each month during dashboard build.
📱
Carousels are the highest-ER Instagram format (avg 6–8% ER) but consistently underused.
Action: Repurpose LinkedIn advisor spotlight documents as Instagram carousels. Target 2+ carousels per month.
🎯
Document posts with CTAs average 20–30% CTR vs 3–5% for videos — the format gap is consistent across all months.
Action: At least one document post per week. Direct challenge hooks ("Most people get X wrong") are the highest-performing format.
🏷️
#FractionalCXO, #PortfolioCareer, #StartupAdvisor — trending niche hashtags rarely used.
Action: Add a 3-hashtag stack to every LinkedIn post. Extends reach to non-followers at zero effort.
🔁
Public LinkedIn mentions receive 0 reshares from the company page — free social proof left on the table every month.
Action: 5-minute weekly habit — check and reshare or comment on company mentions. See Section 9 for this month's confirmed mentions.
🎙️
Authentic people/team content massively outperforms branded content on Instagram.
Action: One team/founder/member short video per month. Record on iPhone, post natively. Sep 25 COO reel hit 22.52% ER — 7× the monthly average.
