Section 0
Headline Findings — June 2026

🔍 Critical Insights — Act on These

⚠️ The Instagram duplicate reel problem happened again. The Founders Community reel was posted June 16 (145 views, 2.75% ER) and June 17 (55 views, 0% ER). The second post received 62% fewer views and zero engagement — algorithm suppression is consistent.
The Deloitte EMEA Tech Fast 500 announcement (June 30) was the standout post of the month — 3,934 impressions, 37 shares, 92 likes, and 7 comments. In a month averaging 1,700 impressions per post, company milestone content drove 2.3× more impressions and 5× more shares than any other post.
LinkedIn impressions dropped 49.7% vs May (27,948 vs 55,609 organic). Three fewer posts and the absence of sponsored promotion explain most of this. However, engagement rate held at 15.77% — close to May's 17.4% — meaning the content quality stayed strong.
Document-style posts continued to dominate clicks: the NED salary calculator (562 clicks, 27.8% CTR) and board advisory assessment (489 clicks, 36.8% CTR) drove 31.4% avg CTR vs 2.5% for videos. Tool-based content with an embedded CTA link is consistently the highest-converting format.
Instagram carousels outperformed reels for the second consecutive month — 5.92% avg ER vs 2.77% for reels. The advisor profiles carousel (June 25) had 9.0% ER and was the only post to generate a save this month.
The giveaway campaign (AI startup stack) ran two near-identical posts on June 9 and June 11 on LinkedIn. Both generated moderate engagement but combined 5,058 impressions suggests audience overlap. One strong, promoted post would likely outperform two organic posts with the same message.

🌱 Trend Gaps — 5 Specific Opportunities
📱
Carousels are outperforming reels on Instagram for the second consecutive month (5.92% vs 2.77% ER) and you're only posting 3/month. Top niche accounts post 4–8 carousels/month and generate 5–10× more saves.
Action: Repurpose your LinkedIn advisor spotlight documents as Instagram carousels — the content is already made.
🎯
You posted 9 times in June vs 12 in May. At your avg 3,104 impressions per post in May, 3 more posts = ~9,300 additional monthly impressions. June's per-post avg was ~3,105 — same quality, just fewer posts.
Action: Add 3–4 posts using repurposed mentor quotes and founder testimonials already in your content library.
🏷️
#FractionalCXO, #PortfolioCareer, and #StartupAdvisor are trending in your niche. The Deloitte announcement used #EMEATechFast500 and drove the most shares — hashtag relevance matters.
Action: Add a 3-hashtag stack to every LinkedIn post. Takes 10 seconds per post.
🔁
The duplicate reel problem occurred again in June (June 16 + 17). This is the third consecutive month with accidental duplicate content on Instagram — it consistently triggers algorithm suppression.
Action: Before posting on Instagram, check your last 24 hours of posts. Set a calendar rule: one post per day maximum.
🎙️
Authentic people content drives disproportionate results. The Deloitte ranking post (CEO quote embedded) was the top performer of the month. Founder-to-camera content and personal milestones outperform polished brand content.
Action: Ask 1 founder/month to record a 30-second selfie video. Use Connectd client wins as the hook.

🔍 Cross-Platform Audience Insight
Your Instagram audience is 64.6% male and concentrated in the 25–44 bracket (71.8% of followers) — professionals still building their careers. Your LinkedIn audience skews decisively more senior: Senior, Director, CXO, and VP levels together represent over 56% of followers (46,081 total).

You're reaching the same professional world at two different career stages. Instagram catches them when they're growing; LinkedIn reaches them when they're deciding. Content strategy should reflect this split: Instagram = career growth, aspiration, and relatable moments; LinkedIn = peer-level expertise, proof, and ROI.

Both audiences are heavily UK-based (Instagram 60.6% UK, LinkedIn ~30% London area), with India and the US as consistent secondary markets — worth targeting for advisor and startup recruitment campaigns.
